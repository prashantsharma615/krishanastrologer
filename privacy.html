Privacy Policy – Krishan Astrologer

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What we collect

Krishan Astrologer asks for your name, date, time and place of birth only to calculate your kundli. All calculations happen on your own device. We do not send this information to any server, we do not store it, and we do not share or sell it to anyone.

Stored on your device

The app saves your language choice and, if you generate a kundli, the birth details you entered (including saved family profiles) in your phone's browser storage, so you don't have to type them again. This data stays on your device. You can delete a profile with the ✕ button, or clear everything by clearing the app's data.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The app has no login, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Nature of the readings

Readings are traditional Vedic astrology (Jyotish) interpretation offered as guidance and for entertainment. They are not guaranteed predictions and are not a substitute for medical, legal or financial advice.
