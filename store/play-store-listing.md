## Full description — English (en-IN)
Krishan Astrologer gives you your Vedic birth chart and practical guidance for life's problems, based on Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS), the classical text of Parashari astrology, together with Mantreswara's Phaladeepika. Available fully in Hindi and English.

★ ACCURATE KUNDLI
• Lagna chart (North Indian style) calculated astronomically — sidereal, Lahiri ayanamsa
• Moon sign, nakshatra and pada, all nine planets with degree, house and dignity
• Vimshottari Mahadasha and Antardasha with exact dates
• Search hundreds of Indian and world cities

★ ASK YOUR PROBLEM
Type your question in your own words — Hindi, English or Hinglish — like "naukri kab lagegi?" or "Why is my marriage delayed?". Every answer has:
1. Problem summary
2. Astrological reasoning from your chart
3. Timing from your dasha
4. Simple, affordable remedies — mantra, charity and fasting day
plus the source chapters from BPHS and Phaladeepika and a "Why?" section that explains each rule used.

★ FUTURE FORECAST
• Next 12 months by pratyantar dasha
• 5-year antardasha timeline with favourable periods and remedies

★ KUNDLI MILAN (Guna Milan)
36-guna Ashtakoot matching with Manglik, Nadi and Bhakoot dosha notes.

★ DAILY RASHIFAL & PANCHANG
Today's tithi, paksha, nakshatra and horoscope for all 12 rashis, with lucky colour, number and remedy.

★ DOSH CHECK
Mangal (Manglik) dosha, Kaal Sarp dosha and Shani Sade Sati / Dhaiya with dates and remedies.

★ MORE
• Save kundlis of your whole family
• Share your kundli on WhatsApp, save as PDF
• Works offline, no login, no ads
• Your birth details stay on your phone

Created by Pallavi Gupta, Founder, Krishan Astrologer.

★ अपनी समस्या पूछें

1. समस्या सारांश
2. आपकी कुंडली से ज्योतिषीय कारण
3. आपकी दशा से सही समय
4. सरल और सस्ते उपाय — मंत्र, दान और व्रत का दिन

★ आज का राशिफल और पंचांग
