1. What this app is

Krishan Astrologer calculates Vedic birth charts and offers interpretations based on classical Jyotish texts, mainly Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra. All readings, forecasts, matching scores and remedies are traditional astrological guidance for spiritual and informational purposes only.

2. No guarantee

Astrology describes tendencies; it cannot guarantee any event or outcome. Do not rely on the app as the only basis for important decisions.

3. Not professional advice

Astrological guidance is for spiritual and informational purposes only and is not a substitute for medical, legal, or financial advice. For health, legal or money matters, always consult a qualified professional. In an emergency, call 112. If you are going through emotional distress, call the Tele-MANAS helpline 14416.

4. Remedies

Suggested remedies (mantra, charity, prayer, fasting) are simple and optional. Gemstones are never required; consult a qualified astrologer before buying one. Do not fast if it is unsafe for your health.

5. Your data

Birth details are processed on your own device and are not sent to any server. See the Privacy Policy.
